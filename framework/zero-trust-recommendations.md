# Zero Trust–Aligned Recommendations

## Recommended controls
- Enforce least privilege with RBAC/ACL tuning and role reviews
- Continuous verification: certificate revalidation and rapid revocation
- Micro-segmentation using channels and private data collections
- Centralized logging and audit trails for accountability
- Policy enforcement aligned to compliance needs (PCI-DSS, SOX, HIPAA)

## Outcome
A practical Zero Trust–aligned framework to strengthen identity governance, access control, auditability, and resilience in enterprise blockchain deployments.
